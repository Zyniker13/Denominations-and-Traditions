# The Book of Concord (1580)

**Status: pending.** The texts will be taken from
[thebookofconcord.org](https://thebookofconcord.org/), whose English text is
the *Concordia Triglotta* translation (St. Louis: Concordia Publishing House,
1921). The site's [legal page](https://thebookofconcord.org/legal/) states that
the Triglotta is no longer under copyright.

Planned contents, one folder per document:

- Preface to the Christian Book of Concord
- The Three Ecumenical Creeds (Apostles', Nicene, Athanasian)
- The Augsburg Confession (1530)
- The Apology of the Augsburg Confession (1531)
- The Smalcald Articles (1537)
- The Treatise on the Power and Primacy of the Pope (1537)
- The Small Catechism (1529)
- The Large Catechism (1529)
- The Formula of Concord: Epitome (1577)
- The Formula of Concord: Solid Declaration (1577)
